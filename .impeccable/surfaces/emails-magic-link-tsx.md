# emails/magic-link

## Scope

Transactional magic-link email. One template for first-time and returning sign-in.

## Visitor mode

Operate

## Audience / job

Signed-out lifter, phone inbox. Job: open the link and get into the app.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A one-action sign-in token on the product’s navy lockup. Refuses the SaaS auth stack (kicker, white card, welcome pitch, three paragraphs, office footer).

OWN-WORLD: Full-bleed `#0A192F`. Identity is the 5×7 contribution heatmap as table cells (email-safe, never SVG). Heat fills `#153048 / #39B3B3 / #73CCCC / #E0FFFF`. Wordmark is geometric mono, bold. Primary action is `#008080` on `#E0FFFF`. Restrained: one accent, no nested cards.

STORY: You asked to sign in. Tap once. First-time and returning share this email. No coaching, no guilt, no product pitch.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Navy field. Heatmap + “Repo” lockup at the top. Heading “Sign in” immediately under it. One short line. Full-width teal button “Sign in”. Quiet fallback URL under the button. That is the entire email.

FORM: Specified-narrow Operate email inside the incumbent product world. Seed: specified.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
